# Customer growth, offers and rewards

Coin redemption is temporarily disabled in cart and checkout through `CHECKOUT_FEATURES.coinRedemption` in `lib/checkout-settings.ts`. The coin controls are hidden and new cart quotes request zero coins. Wallet/admin code and existing saved-order amounts are retained for later completion.

Open http://localhost:3002/admin/growth (also linked as Offers & Rewards). Customers opens the same workspace at its Customers tab. Existing checkout recovery and coupon lead history remain available, with Create offer actions.

- Customers: lifetime orders, cash collected after recorded refunds, average fully paid order value, first/last order, actual verification login and separate account activity; search, segments, date filters, multiple selection, saved groups and CSV. Activity dates do not imply a new login. Historical sessions without an explicit login timestamp use session creation.
- Offers: fixed rupees, percentage, free delivery, free sample; recipient or general code, expiry, minimum spend, usage limits, customer audience, product/category restrictions, percentage cap and welcome-offer stacking. Create & copy message creates a shareable offer. Personal offers require the recipient to verify their account. Reserved unpaid orders consume usage until cancelled.
- WhatsApp: select customers, compose/preview, save draft, schedule or queue after review, inspect individual delivery results and cancel queued recipients. Only opted-in valid Indian mobile numbers are included. Consent is checked again immediately before sending. Account preferences and signed incoming STOP messages revoke consent. Unknown outcomes are not automatically resent.
- Coins: 1 coin = ₹1, prominent brown account card, available/pending/reserved balances and ledger, optional amount in cart/checkout. The database reserves coins with a wallet lock, checks ownership/caps at order creation, spends on recorded payment, and restores after cancellation/verified refunds. Saved orders keep coins reserved; unpaid carts expire after 30 minutes. At least ₹1 merchandise remains payable. Admin manual adjustments require a reason and have idempotency keys.
- Automatic earning and referrals start OFF, as requested. Rates remain editable under Rewards. Enabling earning establishes a start date; existing older orders do not get retrospective rewards. Earned rewards require delivery and full payment, with an editable hold. Referral links require verification, block self-referrals and repeat claims, and reward after delivery/full payment. Reversals void earned rewards. Coins already spent can leave a deficit against future credits.

## Local setup and verification

`npm run db:setup-growth` applies additive, repeatable schema changes. It does not enable rewards or send messages. `npm run test:growth` runs pure rules/pricing tests. `npm run test:growth:integration` creates isolated temporary database fixtures, checks reservation concurrency/ownership/payment/refund idempotency and private offer limits, and removes its fixtures. It never sends messages or changes global reward settings. `npm run test:growth:campaigns` uses a mocked provider to check consent, templates, webhook signatures, delivery ordering and uncertain retries. `npm run test:growth:checkout` requires the local server and checks authenticated quotes and saved orders without taking payments. The optional browser verification scripts require an open Playwright CLI session; their temporary records are cleaned up afterward.

Actual WhatsApp delivery requires WHATSAPP_CLOUD_ACCESS_TOKEN, WHATSAPP_CLOUD_PHONE_NUMBER_ID, WHATSAPP_CLOUD_API_VERSION (explicit version), WHATSAPP_MARKETING_TEMPLATE_NAME and optionally WHATSAPP_MARKETING_TEMPLATE_LANGUAGE (default en). The approved marketing template must contain four body variables, in order: customer name, campaign title, campaign body, campaign link. The Meta account needs marketing-template approval. Existing payment reminder templates are separate.

Configure a public HTTPS webhook at `/api/whatsapp/webhook`, WHATSAPP_WEBHOOK_VERIFY_TOKEN and WHATSAPP_APP_SECRET; subscribe to messages/statuses. The handler verifies the raw-body HMAC and preserves monotonic delivery/read states. Provider acceptance is separate from delivery.

`/api/cron/growth` requires `Authorization: Bearer CRON_SECRET`. A scheduler should invoke it every 10 minutes to process due campaigns (up to 20 recipients per invocation) and reconcile up to 50 customer wallets. Configure a hosting plan/scheduler supporting that cadence; batches can also be processed from the admin campaign screen. The included Vercel configuration declares the schedule; nothing is deployed by these changes.

Development sending stays disabled unless ENABLE_LOCAL_CUSTOMER_NOTIFICATIONS=true. Keep it unset while reviewing locally. Missing credentials/template disable sending but allow drafts/previews. Do not put credentials in client configuration. No real WhatsApp messages were sent during implementation.

The Google business-backup registry includes growth tables, coupon rules and order coin fields, excluding authentication tokens.
