# Saved invoice workspace

The admin invoice workspace is available at `/invoice` and `/bill`. It uses compact labelled inputs on desktop and mobile, multiline addresses and item descriptions, and quick-add controls for perfumes, samples, machines, oils and other business items. Each item can have its own description, unit, quantity, price and tax rate. Free sample lines are supported.

Choose **Save draft** to store every editor field in the database and automatically remember reusable customer/item/business details. **Saved invoices** lets you search by customer, save name, invoice number or item description. **Open** lets you update the existing saved record. **Use as new** copies its details into an unsaved invoice with a fresh number and date and resets payment status to Unpaid. **Save as copy** creates a separate record from the current edited form. Copies do not overwrite their source.

Saving is explicit. Unsaved changes trigger a warning before leaving or replacing the form. Changes from another tab/device are detected on update and rejected instead of silently overwritten. Server-side admin authentication protects saved customer data and the workspace is excluded from indexing.

Schema setup: `npx tsx scripts/apply-saved-invoices-schema.ts`. This creates only the new `saved_invoices` table, with no changes to orders or products. It has been applied to the configured database. If deployment uses a different database, run it there before using invoice saving.

Checks: `node --import tsx --test tests/invoice-document.test.ts`, `npx tsc --noEmit`, and `npx tsx scripts/check-saved-invoices.ts`. The database check saves, updates and reopens a synthetic record inside a transaction that is rolled back, leaving no test invoice behind.

## Drafts, issue register and PDF export

New work uses a draft reference until **Issue invoice & lock details** is selected. The server allocates `HF/2627/000001`-style consecutive numbers inside a financial-year transaction, writes an immutable issue snapshot, and records revisions. The three additive tables are prepared by `node --import tsx scripts/apply-invoice-ledger.ts` using `sql/add_invoice_ledger.sql`. Existing saved documents are not rewritten or retroactively marked issued. The first subsequent edit retains their prior state as a legacy snapshot. New deployment environments need the additive migration before invoice saving/issuing is used.

Issued customer, item, tax and identity fields cannot be edited through the API or editor. Manual payment records/visibility can be updated, with history; this does not collect or refund money. Use Records & corrections to create credit notes, record completed refunds or cancel eligible unpaid invoices. Debit notes are not implemented.

`lib/invoice-calculations.ts` is shared by the editor, saved register, server validation and PDF. Prices can be inclusive or exclusive of GST. Invoice-time target-total discounts are allocated to item gross values in paise before recomputing tax; shipping remains outside that discount and has an explicit reviewed GST rate. Invalid targets/overpayments are blocked. Rounding preserves the sum of taxable value and tax, with any odd paise assigned consistently between CGST/SGST. GST rates/HSN/SAC are entered by the operator, not inferred from product names.

Tax treatment supports domestic CGST/SGST, IGST, and an explicit No GST option. Place of supply must be determined by the operator; it is not inferred solely from the shipping address or buyer GSTIN. GSTIN format/state and HSN presence checks are structural, not verification against the GST portal. Export/SEZ/LUT, reverse-charge handling, debit-note generation, digital signatures, e-invoice IRNs/e-way bills and GST return filing are outside this implementation. Confirm applicable requirements with the accountant before issuing real documents.

The workspace Download PDF action captures the displayed invoice itself with its loaded website fonts, then embeds that high-resolution rendering into A4 pages. It does not redraw the invoice, substitute fonts or add headers, footers, page numbers, labels or rules. Long previews are split before rows/paragraphs where possible. This appearance-preserving download is image-backed rather than selectable PDF text. Filenames use the invoice date, IST export time, customer and HUME Fragrance. The server-side PDF endpoint remains a separate internal renderer; the workspace no longer uses it for Download PDF.

Both Download PDF and Copy Image use the same preview capture helper. The helper waits for fonts/images, preserves the computed font family and current preview width, and cleans up its off-screen clone. Save pending changes to an issued payment record before downloading. Preview dirty checks compare stable key ordering so JSONB serialization does not falsely report unsaved changes.

Item search uses the current INR catalogue (including hidden catalogue entries for the authenticated admin) and generic unpriced oil/diffuser presets. Selecting a suggestion fills a blank line or adds a line; catalogue prices remain editable and no tax classification is inferred. There is no Google or external search request. The price selector includes an invoice-wide **No GST charged** option backed by `taxMode: none`: calculations charge zero tax and the preview/PDF hide GSTIN, HSN, state-of-supply tax details, tax columns and tax breakdowns. Existing tax settings remain stored for switching back. This is an operator-selected presentation/calculation mode, not a determination of statutory exemption; supplier purchase-tax treatment and selling price do not establish exemption.

Editor sections form a single-open accordion, initially focused on Items. Changing sections preserves field values. Bespoke descriptions (including typed sizes such as `White tea fragrance oil (100gm)`) are remembered after leaving the item row, or immediately with **Save suggestion**. Only item name, category, unit and price are saved; customer information and invoice status are not copied. Existing catalogue/preset entries keep their catalogue prices. Saved custom suggestions persist in `invoice_item_suggestions` across sessions/devices; normalized name + unit prevents duplicate entries and re-saving updates the reusable price. Failures show a retry option while preserving the current invoice. Issued invoice locks remain in force.

Prepare the additive suggestion table with `node --import tsx scripts/apply-invoice-item-suggestions.ts` (applied to the configured database). `scripts/verify-invoice-item-suggestions.ts` checks authenticated API persistence, reload and duplicate updates in a rolled-back transaction. No products or invoices are changed by this migration or verification.

References: [CBIC invoice rules](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content-page/explore-rules/1000136/1000001) and [CGST Section 15, value of taxable supply](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content-page/explore-act/1000284/1000001).

Checks: `node --import tsx --test tests/invoice-accounting.test.ts tests/invoice-document.test.ts tests/invoice-filename.test.ts`. `scripts/verify-invoice-ledger.ts` wraps every write, audit entry and number allocation in a rolled-back transaction. `scripts/render-invoice-fixtures.ts` creates synthetic PDF fixtures for Poppler review, without saving or issuing customer invoices.

## Mobile workspace and draft recovery

Below the desktop split-view breakpoint, Edit invoice / Preview invoice switches between the existing editor and the A4 document. Preview fits the screen with a full-size option; capture always uses the original unscaled paper dimensions. Phones use invoice cards instead of the wide register table and compact controls.

Unsaved edits are backed up after 600 ms in this tab's sessionStorage. Reloading offers Restore draft / Discard backup. Backups retain the saved ID and expected update timestamp, so restoring does not bypass concurrent-edit protection. This is tab-local recovery, not cross-device or offline database saving. Saving or starting a clean invoice clears the backup. Browser storage failures leave explicit saving available.

Business, bank, terms and tax/display defaults are remembered centrally on invoice save, with local fallback. Customer search uses the authenticated, server-searched directory. Recently selected catalogue items rise to the top of item search during the editor session. Validation errors provide links to open the section and focus the affected field.

## Customer directory, full register and corrections

Setup in a new environment: `node --import tsx scripts/apply-invoice-management.ts`. The migration adds customer profiles, invoice/customer links, a correction counter and append-only correction records, plus register indexes. It does not rewrite, issue or cancel existing invoices. It has been applied to the configured database.

**Customers** opens a searchable, paginated directory with create/edit forms. Selecting a directory customer in the editor copies their billing details into the draft and retains the customer ID. Saving links the draft to that customer. Profile edits do not rewrite old invoices. Older saved invoices are deliberately not matched by name: use **Saved invoices → Records → Customer statement link** to explicitly assign them. The unlinked-only register filter helps find them; assignments are audited and use stale-update protection.

**Statement & balance** shows all linked documents, paginated 25 at a time. Totals include only issued documents and include all linked issued invoices, credits, recorded received amounts and completed refunds. The full CSV contains every linked document and correction, with spreadsheet-formula escaping for textual fields. A negative net customer balance means customer credit owed. Drafts and unlinked documents do not contribute. A legacy Paid/Partially Paid label without a known received amount makes the customer balance Unknown until its actual receipt amount is recorded. Received amounts are current manually entered totals, not a dated payment transaction ledger.

The saved register now searches and filters in PostgreSQL, with deterministic sorting and pagination. It returns 25 documents per page by default (maximum 100), plus the full match count. No latest-500 cap remains. It supports customer/name/contact/number/item search, draft/issued/cancelled filters, manual payment labels, amount/date/update sorting and unlinked invoices. Counts and page contents share one read snapshot; requests are debounced and stale responses aborted.

**Records & corrections** offers three permanent, reviewed operations on issued invoices. Credit notes reverse selected quantities using their original allocated discounts and taxes; cumulative paise rounding ensures partial credits can never exceed the source quantities or value. Original shipping may be reversed once. A refund records a transfer already completed, including its method/reference; it is capped by credit owed from actual receipts, and does not call a payment gateway. Cancellation creates a full credit note, retains the original invoice/number, and locks further accounting edits. It is restricted to invoices with zero recorded receipts and no prior corrections; paid returns use credit notes and refund records. These are internal accounting records and do not submit documents or cancellations to a tax portal.

Corrections have a server-allocated financial-year reference, issue snapshot, reason, date and revision entry. They cannot be edited/deleted through the API. All writes share the invoice lock, expected-update checks reject concurrent stale writes, and stable operation IDs make retries idempotent. Corrections advance the invoice revision timestamp without changing its issued financial details. Receipt edits after a correction must keep explicit payment amounts enabled and cannot reduce receipts below completed refunds. Credit notes and refund records have authenticated PDF downloads with source invoice references and tax breakdowns; the existing invoice preview/PDF design is unchanged.

Verification: `node --import tsx --test tests/invoice-management.test.ts`, `node --import tsx scripts/verify-invoice-management.ts`, and `node --import tsx scripts/render-correction-fixtures.ts`. The database verification wraps customer edits, 550 synthetic invoices, links, corrections and sequence increments in a rolled-back transaction. The PDF script writes synthetic examples only under `output/pdf/`.

## Automatic reuse on invoice save

Saving or issuing a draft now atomically remembers its customer, line-item suggestions and business defaults. New customer profiles are created and linked without a separate directory form. Repeat entries match normalized GSTIN, or name plus matching contact/address details; selected directory IDs are retained. Nonempty saved details update the reusable profile, while blank optional fields do not erase existing profile data. Issued payment updates do not rewrite reusable profiles or defaults from historical invoices.

Every saved item is remembered by normalized description + unit, including its latest entered price, category, HSN/SAC, GST rate and price/tax mode. Invoice quantities and discounts are not reused. Suggestions refresh after saving; selected prices are converted between inclusive/exclusive modes when needed. The separate Save suggestion control is removed. This does not change storefront catalogue prices.

Business identity, bank details, terms and tax/display preferences are saved centrally and loaded for a fresh untouched invoice, with local business defaults as fallback. Customer-specific addresses, supply details, notes, payments and target totals are excluded from these defaults. The separate Remember defaults control is removed. Setup: `node --import tsx scripts/apply-invoice-reuse.ts` (applied to the configured database). Existing invoices are unchanged. Verification now also covers automatic customer creation/matching, repeat-save idempotency, item metadata persistence and reusable defaults within the rollback transaction.
